# PaceKeeper AI – Help

PaceKeeper AI combines training planning, completed activities, wellness and
performance data, and nutrition planning. It synchronises with
**intervals.icu** and can be assisted by its integrated AI or external AI
connectors.

> [!WARNING]
> **Alpha version:** PaceKeeper AI is under active development. Features,
> icons, and workflows may change. Always review AI suggestions before applying
> them.

## Recommended order for your first setup

1. Sign in with intervals.icu and complete the setup assistant.
2. Become familiar with Dashboard, readiness, and the calendar.
3. Create your first plan with goals, availability, and events.
4. Generate the plan phases first, then the workouts for every phase.
5. Preferably plan nutrition one calendar week at a time.
6. Connect Claude as an external AI assistant when needed.
