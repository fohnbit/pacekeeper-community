1. Sign in through **intervals.icu**.
2. PaceKeeper AI imports available profile information from intervals.icu.
3. Review and complete it in the setup assistant that opens automatically.
4. Save the summary and wait for the first synchronisation.
5. Review your day in **Dashboard** and your calendar under **Plan**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Imported intervals.icu values are a starting point, not a guarantee that
> they are correct. Missing or incorrect information reduces the quality of
> later training and nutrition suggestions.

## Information collected by the setup assistant

First select whether you want to use PaceKeeper AI for **Training**,
**Nutrition**, or both. This determines which pages the assistant displays.

### Personal data

- name, gender, and date of birth
- height and weight
- automatic weight synchronisation or a weekly reminder

### Daily life and training

- work days and usual working hours
- shift work
- sports you actually practise
- training goal

Working hours help PaceKeeper AI fit workouts and meals realistically into
your day. For shift work, the app regularly asks for the current week's
schedule.

### Nutrition

- nutrition goal and additional focus areas
- diet type
- meat and fish frequency
- cuisine and preparation preferences
- allergies and intolerances
- dislikes and preferences
- meals to plan and their usual times

Check allergies and intolerances especially carefully. AI-generated plans are
not a substitute for medical or nutritional advice.

## Reopening setup or the introduction

- **Settings → Setup assistant** opens profile setup again.
- **Settings → Replay tutorial** repeats the short Dashboard introduction.
